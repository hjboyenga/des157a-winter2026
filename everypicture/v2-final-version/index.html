1984

Before Us

This glacier formed over ten thousand years. It existed long before our cities, our machines, and our fires.

Invisible Hands

No one decided to melt this glacier. And yet here we are billions of small decisions, burning fuel, clearing land, adding warmth reshaping something ancient without ever touching it.

The Retreat Continues

For most of history, only volcanoes and ice ages could change a landscape this dramatically. Now we can.

Accelerating Loss

We built highways, not this. We heated homes, not this. And yet this is what we made. The most profound changes humans cause are rarely the ones we planned.

Unintended

Every civilization leaves a mark on the Earth. This is ours. Not carved in stone, but carved in absence.

1984

Nothing Here

Bare desert. Rock and sand baking under the Mojave sun. Nature spent millions of years deciding nothing should grow here. We looked at it and saw something else entirely.

Rewriting the Rules

We imported water from hundreds of miles away, pumped it into fountains, filled swimming pools, and kept the lights on all night. We didn't adapt to the desert. We simply overruled it.

The Same Hands

The same species that accidentally erased a glacier deliberately built a city where nothing could survive. Will we be the cause of our own destruction?
