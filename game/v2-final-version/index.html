HOW TO PLAY

  • Take turns rolling 2 dice each round.
  • Add your roll to your score and move forward.
  • Roll a 1 on either die - Cramp! Turn over, no points!
  • Roll (1+1) False Start: Back to the Blocks! - your score resets to zero!
  • First player to reach 40 points wins the race!
